### How to use this checklist
Each item must be manually confirmed or confirmed by CI before cutting the `v1.0.0-rc` tag. Items marked `- [x]` are confirmed. Items left as `- [ ]` are open. Do not cut the tag until all items in `Core functionality`, `Test coverage`, and `Release mechanics` are checked. `Known limitations` items are documentation tasks, not blockers.
