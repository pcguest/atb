# Founder Outbound Playbook

This playbook is for founder-led distribution in the next 30 to 90 days.

The goal is not broad awareness. The goal is to find teams that already feel the pain ATB is built for.

## The Wedge

Lead with this:

`ATB gives teams a portable, tamper-evident record of AI workflow behaviour they can verify locally and hand to reviewers without depending on a vendor control plane.`

Do not lead with:

- observability
- self-hosting
- compliance
- dashboards

Those are supporting details, not the wedge.

## Primary Segments

### 1. Security-Minded Internal AI Teams

Look for:

- internal copilots
- support agents
- knowledge agents
- workflows touching customer or employee data

Pain signal:

- they can debug, but they cannot produce a trustworthy post-incident record without sending traces to a vendor

### 2. Consultancies and Delivery Teams

Look for:

- firms shipping bespoke AI workflows into customer environments
- teams working with regulated buyers
- builders who need handoff artefacts after delivery

Pain signal:

- handoff currently depends on screenshots, ad hoc notes, or continued platform access

### 3. Privacy-Sensitive Enterprise Builders

Look for:

- legal, healthcare, fintech, insurance, government-adjacent, or enterprise IT teams
- strong internal review processes
- risk or procurement pressure around external trace storage

Pain signal:

- hosted tracing is blocked, slowed, or politically difficult

## Weekly Cadence

### Monday

- build a fresh list of 10 to 15 accounts
- identify one relevant workflow for each account
- send 5 highly targeted messages

### Tuesday

- send 5 more messages
- follow up on prior replies
- tighten examples based on objections

### Wednesday

- run discovery calls
- capture direct language from buyers
- note whether the pain is incident review, customer handoff, or private audit

### Thursday

- send follow-ups and one concrete example to warm accounts
- propose working sessions or a paid pilot where fit is strong

### Friday

- review what actually moved conversations
- remove weak segments
- update copy with buyer language, not founder assumptions

## Qualification Rules

Spend time only where all three are true:

1. the workflow is real now
2. local control or vendor independence matters
3. a portable evidence artefact would remove a real blocker

If any of those are missing, move on.

## Evidence To Collect

By the end of discovery, you should know:

- who reviews the evidence today
- what artefact they currently receive
- why the current artefact fails
- whether the buyer wants a control plane or a handoff artefact
- whether a paid pilot is realistic this quarter

## Anti-Patterns

Do not spend cycles on:

- teams mainly shopping for prompt management
- eval-first buyers
- hobbyist agents
- anyone who clearly wants a collaborative cloud control plane first

## Success Criteria

After 10 to 15 real conversations, you should know:

- which use case converts best
- whether secure handoff is a must-have or just interesting
- whether buyers will pay for a narrow pilot

If you do not know those answers, the problem is not roadmap breadth. The problem is weak qualification.
